Mindoku is an AI-powered Sudoku experience designed as a gentle alternative to digital overstimulation. Built for students and young professionals living in a world of constant dopamine-driven distractions, it replaces mindless scrolling with structured focus. Instead of starting the day with noise and instant gratification, users engage in calm, intentional problem-solving that helps the brain wake up with clarity. Through AI-guided hints, reflective feedback, and focus coaching, Mindoku turns each puzzle into a moment of mental training — not just for logic, but for attention, discipline, and cognitive wellbeing.

🚀 Mindoku — AI-Powered Sudoku Focus Trainer

🧠 About the Project

Mindoku is not just a Sudoku game — it is an AI-powered cognitive training experience designed to improve focus, logical thinking, and mental endurance through adaptive gameplay and intelligent coaching.

Instead of simply solving puzzles, users train their attention, track their progress, and receive real-time AI guidance that helps them understand their thinking patterns.

⸻

🎯 Problem

Modern students and young professionals face:

* Decreased attention span due to digital overload
* Difficulty maintaining deep focus
* Lack of engaging cognitive training tools

Mindoku transforms Sudoku into a focus-building system, not just a game.

⸻

✨ Key Features

🎮 Smart Sudoku Gameplay

* Fully playable Sudoku board
* Multiple difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert
* Timer for focus tracking
* Restart & reset functionality

✍️ Notes Mode

* Mark possible numbers
* Plan strategies like a real Sudoku player

⚡ Instant Mistake Detection

* Real-time validation of moves
* Helps users learn patterns instead of guessing

🤖 AI Guided Assistance

* AI explains why a move is wrong
* Provides intelligent hints instead of direct answers
* Encourages logical reasoning development

🧘 AI Focus Coach

* Motivational, context-aware messages during gameplay
* Encourages deep focus and reduces frustration
* Builds consistent cognitive habits

🔥 Focus Streak System

* Tracks daily play consistency
* Encourages habit formation and discipline

🎉 Victory Experience

* Smooth animation upon puzzle completion
* Reinforces achievement and motivation

⸻

🎨 Design Philosophy

* Glassmorphic, premium UI inspired by modern Apple-style design
* Dark-mode focused interface for deep concentration
* Minimalist line icons for clarity
* Smooth micro-animations for feedback and immersion

⸻

🛠 Tech Stack

* Frontend: Next.js, TypeScript
* Styling: Tailwind CSS
* AI Integration: Gemini API + Genkit
* Architecture: Component-based, scalable React structure

⸻

🧩 AI Philosophy

Mindoku uses AI not to “solve the game for the user”, but to:

* Guide thinking processes
* Detect patterns in mistakes
* Encourage cognitive improvement
* Act as a personal focus coach

The goal is learning through interaction, not automation.
